# WSET Systematic Approach to Tasting as User Standard

Vinora adopts the **WSET Systematic Approach to Tasting (SAT)** as the standard framework for all user wine evaluation and review structure. The SAT's four-phase framework (Appearance, Nose, Palate, Conclusions) and standardized vocabulary provide users with a professional yet accessible language for describing wines. User reviews are mapped to WSET scales through AI extraction, bridging casual tasting notes with structured, comparable data.

**Considered Options:**
- **Free-form reviews only** — Simplest but creates unstructured, uncomparable data
- **Custom Vinora taxonomy** — Reinventing the wheel; no external legitimacy
- **WSET SAT** — Industry-recognized, comprehensive, scales from beginner to professional

**Decision Rationale:**
- WSET is the global standard for wine education (90+ countries, 100K+ students annually)
- The SAT framework scales from Level 2 (beginner) to Level 4 (expert)
- Standardized vocabulary enables meaningful comparison across reviews
- Professional credibility attracts serious wine enthusiasts while remaining accessible

**Consequences:**
- All review prompts and AI extraction align with WSET terminology
- Wine detail pages display structural attributes using WSET scales
- User education: tooltips explain WSET terms (e.g., "What does 'Medium(+) acidity mean?")
- Future: Vinora could offer WSET-style tasting courses as premium feature
- Spanish market adaptation: WSET terms coexist with traditional Spanish terminology (crianza, reserva, etc.)
